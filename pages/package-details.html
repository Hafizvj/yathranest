Kerala Package

Magical Kerala

Destination: Munnar · Thekkady · Alleppey Duration: 5 Days / 4 Nights

A classic Kerala circuit combining misty tea hills, wildlife landscapes and a peaceful overnight houseboat stay.

Request Pricing Enquire Now

Overview

Designed for first-time visitors and families, Magical Kerala balances scenic drives, gentle sightseeing and unhurried evenings. Share your travel dates and we’ll tailor stays and pacing before sending personalised pricing.

Trip Highlights

  • Tea plantation views in Munnar
  • Spice garden visit near Thekkady
  • Overnight Alleppey houseboat experience
  • Flexible photo stops and leisure time
  • Private cab for sightseeing segments

Detailed Itinerary

Meet our representative, drive to Munnar with scenic stops. Evening at leisure in the hills.

Visit viewpoints, tea museum / plantation areas and local markets. Optional activities on request.

Drive to Thekkady. Spice plantation visit and optional boat ride / cultural show (subject to availability).

Board a traditional houseboat. Cruise the canals, enjoy onboard meals and overnight on the waters.

Breakfast on board, disembark and transfer to Cochin for onward journey.

Inclusions

  • Accommodation as per itinerary
  • Daily breakfast (and houseboat meals as applicable)
  • Private transfers for sightseeing segments
  • Assistance at key points

Exclusions

  • Flights / trains
  • Personal expenses & optional activities
  • Entry fees not mentioned
  • Anything not listed in inclusions

Accommodation

Comfortable hotels / resorts in Munnar and Thekkady, plus an overnight houseboat stay in Alleppey. Specific properties are confirmed based on availability for your dates.

Transportation

Private air-conditioned cab for transfers and sightseeing as outlined. Vehicle category can be adjusted based on group size when you enquire.
